# Silvertongue

A private, PIN-locked training course for the spoken (and written) word: persuasion, presence,
leading conversations, attraction, tension, anticipation, direct and indirect influence, texting,
and spotting manipulation. Unlisted: reachable by its link only.

- **9 modules, 63 techniques.** Each has a difficulty rating, an effectiveness rating, an evidence
  label (research-backed, practitioner-proven or anecdotal), direct/indirect tag, sources, why it
  works, steps, common mistakes, an ethics line, 6 to 10 "flat → crafted" examples (read aloud on
  tap), rewrite drills with model answers and two scenario quizzes.
- **Learn:** rank (Apprentice → Silvertongue), XP, streak, today's technique, module progress.
- **Browse:** filter by module and direct/indirect; sort by best value, most effective, easiest, hardest.
- **Practise:** 8-question rounds weighted toward what you miss; the rewrite gym.
- **Journal:** field notes of real uses (+ mastery stage 3).
- **PIN lock:** 4-digit PIN stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash; locks on open and after a minute
  away, hides content in the app switcher, 30 s wait after 5 wrong tries, “Forgot?” erases.
- Progress backup/restore in Settings. Everything stays on the device.

Content: researched and written for this app (`data/lessons.json`); all example lines are original.
Ethics: influence that works with people. Coercive tactics appear only in the Shields module, as
things to recognise and counter.
