# Silvertongue

A private, PIN-locked training course for the spoken (and written) word: persuasion, presence,
leading conversations, attraction, tension, anticipation, direct and indirect influence, texting,
and spotting manipulation. Unlisted: reachable by its link only.

- **12 modules, 91 lessons:** Presence & Listening, Persuasion, Leading the Conversation, Storytelling, Ericksonian Language (truisms, yes-set, pacing and leading, permissive suggestion, embedded commands, presuppositions, double binds, nested stories, utilization; honestly rated), Attraction & Flirting, Subtlety & Implication, Tension & Polarity, Anticipation & Desire, Direct & Indirect, Texting & Written Words, Shields. Each has a difficulty rating, an effectiveness rating, an evidence
  label (research-backed, practitioner-proven or anecdotal), direct/indirect tag, sources, why it
  works, steps, common mistakes, an ethics line (*emphasis* marked in gold) and 6 to 10 "flat → crafted" examples (read aloud on tap, filterable by context).
- **Adult modules (18+), hidden by default:** Sex Talk & Dirty Talk, and Command & Lead (18 techniques, 144 examples), consensual and frank but not graphic, with negotiation, check-ins and aftercare built in. Turned on in Settings after an 18+ confirmation; they live in `data/adult.json`, which is only downloaded once switched on.
- **Lessons:** modules, then lessons, plus search. Each lesson is just the technique, how to do it and its examples; why it works, mistakes and sources fold away.
- **Notebook:** every lesson has its own lined notebook page that saves as you type. The Notebook tab lists your pages, newest first, with search. Notes from older versions move onto their lesson's page.
- **PIN lock:** 4-digit PIN stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash; locks on open and after a minute
  away, hides content in the app switcher, 30 s wait after 5 wrong tries, “Forgot?” erases.
- Notebook backup/restore in Settings. Everything stays on the device.

Content: researched and written for this app (`data/lessons.json`); all example lines are original.
Ethics: influence that works with people. Coercive tactics appear only in the Shields module, as
things to recognise and counter.
