# Attune

Genuine empathy, not performed niceness: attention, listening, seeing through other eyes, kindness in action, empathy
with the people closest to you, and empathy as a lifelong practice. 30 principles, each taught through two true tales
(60 in all) told poetically in plain words, with a note page for every principle.

- **Six books:** The Ground, Listening, Through Other Eyes, Empathy in Action, The Close Ones, The Deep Practice.
- **Each principle:** motto, essence, two tales, the deeper why (psychology, neuroscience and philosophy, with honest notes on debated findings), four practices (some with Philippine touches: kapwa, pakikiramdam, lamay, the mano) and its shadow (empathic distress, bias toward people like us, manipulation, losing yourself, fixing instead of feeling).
- **Notes:** a floating ✎ Note button on every principle and tale; Notebook tab with search; Tales tab with search; backup and restore.

Content in `data/attune.json` is original writing; quotations come only from public-domain texts or short documented
words, and legends, parables and composites are labelled as such. Its rulebook lives under `canon` (harmony check).
