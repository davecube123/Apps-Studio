# Lifeline

First aid for laypeople, built to be used in the moment and learned beforehand.

- **Emergency:** a one-tap call button (number set by country, Philippines 911 by default; Thailand 1669 and others in Settings) and 38 emergency cards, life-threatening first. Each card has big numbered steps you tap off, what not to do and when to call. The screen stays awake.
- **CPR beat:** 110 per minute with sound and a pulsing circle; hands-only or 30:2 with a two-breath prompt.
- **Timers:** cooling a burn (20 min), flushing chemicals, washing a bite (15 min), timing a seizure (5 min), anaphylaxis second dose, low blood sugar recheck, and more.
- **Learn:** 51 lessons in five areas: life support, bleeding and wounds, medical emergencies, injuries and burns, heat/water/bites (rabies, snakes, box jellyfish, marine stings) plus mental health crisis and emergency childbirth. Each: signs, steps, don'ts, when to call, children/pregnancy notes, why it works, sources. A note page per lesson.
- **Practice:** 153 scenario questions; rounds of 10 put what you haven't mastered first; mastery per lesson.
- **Kit:** a checklist of 25 items and local emergency numbers.

Content follows current consensus (ILCOR, AHA and American Red Cross, ERC, IFRC 2020, WHO). It is no substitute for a hands-on course or professional care.
