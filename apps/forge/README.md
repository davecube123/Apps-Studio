# Forge

A private daily training app (unlisted: deliberately not in the studio index or README table, at the owner's
request). Knowledge, action and reflection for seduction, sexual skill and sexual energy, with XP, ranks and streaks.

- **Today:** rank and XP, a daily plan (one lesson, one field mission, one drill or habit at your level), five energy
  habits, a field report, a field-session tally for nights out, and a breathing state reset.
- **Learn:** 50 short lessons in 8 modules (mindset, attraction, conversation, reading her, logistics, threesomes,
  energy, the craft), each with research findings and sources, field notes, one action and one reflection question;
  24 scenario quizzes, including calibration items where the right answer is to slow down or stop.
- **Train:** 54 missions (field, drill, habit) by level 1 to 5, a guided pelvic floor coach whose holds lengthen
  with practice, and a breathing timer.
- **Log:** field reports, a 14-day approach chart, conversion rates, and the most common sticking point linked to a lesson.

The line it holds: every skill aims at mutual, enthusiastic consent (both women, in a threesome); hesitation is
information, not an obstacle; safer sex is part of the craft. Content is original; claims cite published research,
and weak evidence is labelled as such. Everything is stored on the device. Fonts: Fraunces, Inter (SIL OFL).
