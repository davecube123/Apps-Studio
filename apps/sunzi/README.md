# Sunzi 孫子兵法

Sunzi's *Art of War* for skilful living and making things happen.

- **Tales:** 65 tales, five per chapter, from history and legend across China, Japan, India, Persia, the Islamic world, Africa, Europe and the Americas (Sima Qian, the Three Kingdoms, Herodotus, Plutarch, Aesop, the Panchatantra, Rumi, the Baburnama, the Secret History of the Mongols…), retold in new words, each linked to the verses it brings to life. Tales tab with region filters, a tale of the day, "A tale" buttons on verses, the lesson behind a tap with the linked verses quoted, a question for your life, read-aloud.
- **Text:** all 13 chapters, 152 verses. The classical Chinese (public domain) is hidden by default and can be switched on in Settings; with it off, no Chinese characters are shown anywhere.
- **Rendering:** an original scholarly and poetic English rendering written for this app from the Chinese, with key terms (勢, 形, 奇正, 虛實…) glossed in pinyin.
- **For living:** a reflection on every verse that turns the strategy into everyday wisdom: work, leadership, money, negotiation, relationships, habits, creativity.
- **Each chapter:** introduction, one-line essence, three weekly practices (tick them off), read-aloud, "apply it in the journal".
- **籤 Draw:** shake the bamboo-slip tube for a maxim of the day; share it.
- **藏 Saved** verses, **策 Journal** (situation → principle → your move), search across the rendering and reflections.
- Show or hide the Chinese and the reflections; Bamboo and Lacquer-night themes; text size; install prompt; offline.
- Fonts: Cinzel, Cormorant Garamond and LXGW WenKai TC subset to the characters of the text (SIL OFL).
