# Psyche

An encyclopedia of 100 key findings in psychology, written plainly for curious non-specialists, with an honest answer
to one question for each: does it hold up?

- **Eight areas:** Memory and Learning, Attention and Perception, Decisions and Biases, Social Influence, Motivation and
  Habits, Emotion and Stress, Love and Relationships, Self and Wellbeing.
- **Each entry:** the idea, the key study told as a short story, the finding, a rating (holds up, mixed evidence, did
  not replicate) with what replications and reviews found up to 2026, everyday uses, myth and truth, related entries,
  the original reference and a quiz question.
- **Explore** (finding of the day, evidence summary, areas, search), **A to Z** with evidence filters, **Quiz** (questions
  you miss come back more often), **Notebook** (bookmarks and a note page per entry), backup and restore.

Content in `data/psyche.json` is original writing summarising published research; references name the original papers.
Its rulebook lives under `canon` (harmony check). Evidence ratings are judgements and science moves on: check the
original papers for anything important.
