# Plenty

Wealth and abundance, honestly: how wealth is really built (earning, keeping, growing, giving) and what true wealth is
(health, time, people, enough). 30 principles, each taught through two tales (60 in all) told poetically in plain
words, with a note page for every principle.

- **Six books:** The Abundant Mind, The Earning, The Keeping, The Growing, The Flowing, True Wealth.
- **Each principle:** motto, essence, two tales, the deeper why (economics, behavioural finance and psychology with honest notes on debated findings), four practices (some with Philippine examples: paluwagan, Pag-IBIG MP2, SSS, five-six loans) and its shadow (greed, hoarding, recklessness, neglecting people).
- **No promises of returns and no product or stock picks.** Luck and risk are named plainly.
- **Notes:** a floating ✎ Note button on every principle and tale; Notebook tab with search; Tales tab with search; backup and restore.

Content in `data/plenty.json` is original writing; quotations come only from public-domain texts, and legends and composites are labelled as such.
