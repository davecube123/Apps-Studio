# Kalinga

A Taglish guide for the family caring at home for a young man with psychosis in El Nido, Palawan, written from a
friend who cares. It starts from respect for the family and the weight of what they are doing, then teaches in
short pages: what is really happening (not laziness, not drugs, not their fault), what to say instead of scolding,
how to calm themselves first, what to tell neighbours, and why to join his 17 November consult. Installable, offline PWA.

Built from the same base as Kasama (his girlfriend's app), but a separate app with its own content and storage, so
each stays private to its users.

- *Home:* one small way a day to be close to him (warmth at home protects against relapse), a log of good moments,
  the next consult, and an 8-step path (understand, a calm week, join the consult, notes, medicine, costs, a plan, self-care).
- *Unawain:* 14 short lessons, beginning with "Hindi ito katamaran, droga, o kahinaan", plus a calm home,
  "Kapag naiinis o nagagalit na kayo", why to join the doctor, what to tell neighbours, faith, safety and recovery.
- *Kausapin:* LEAP and scripts for family situations (wanting to lecture him, talking back, suspecting drugs,
  disagreeing about him, "I'm not sick", voices, withdrawal, medicine, after an incident), and 9 practice scenarios.
- *Tulong:* emergency numbers and El Nido Community Hospital, El Nido and Palawan contacts, step-by-step guides
  (AICS, free DOH medicines, PWD ID, Malasakit, PhilHealth), a plan for heavy days, and a doctor-visit summary.
- *Para sa inyo:* check-in, a daily psalm (Ang Biblia 1905 and WEB), and pages for parents and for siblings.
- Optional PIN. Everything stays on the phone.
