# Keel

Self-reliance in all its aspects, so you can live with equanimity and fullness. Like a keel, it keeps you upright
in any weather. 30 principles, each taught through two tales (60 in all) told poetically in plain words, with a
note page for every principle.

- **Six books:** The Inner Citadel (mind), The Able Hands (skills, food, body, learning, readiness), The Free Purse (money and time), The Steady Heart (emotions), The Open Hand (interdependence, bayanihan, boundaries, asking for help, service), The Quiet Fullness (presence, mortality, nature, practice, loving your fate).
- **Each principle:** motto, essence, two tales, the deeper why (Stoic, Epicurean, Buddhist, Taoist, Emerson and Thoreau, plus psychology with honest notes on debated findings), four practices for this week and its shadow (where self-reliance turns into pride or isolation).
- **Notes:** a floating ✎ Note button on every principle and tale; Notebook tab with search; Tales tab with search; backup and restore.

Content in `data/keel.json` is original writing; quotations come only from public-domain texts, and legends are labelled as such.
