# Anchor

A private guide for the owner. Unlisted (not in the studio index or README table; noindex). All content is
AES-256-GCM encrypted in `data/anchor.enc.json` (key from the owner's 8-digit passcode via PBKDF2-SHA256, unlocked in
the app by `lib/vault.js`, with an optional quick PIN per phone); the plain source is kept out of the repo.

Thirty principles of dominance in seven books, in the same format as Allure, Trine and Sprezzatura: the core (the
ground of authority, the covenant of consent, structure and discipline, scenes and aftercare, desire and anticipation)
and its two wings, Daddy (care and little space, always non-sexual) and Master (service and protocol). Each principle
has a motto, its essence, four techniques, two true tales, the deeper why, practices for the week, the shadow and the
owner's own notes. Adult to adult only; consent, the signals (Red, Yellow, Green; three taps or a dropped object) and
aftercare run through everything.

Since v18 the earlier planner and journal (limits, rules, rituals, journal notes) are retired. Their records stay on
the phone under the old storage key and can be downloaded from Settings. Fonts: Cormorant Garamond (SIL OFL).
