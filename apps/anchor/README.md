# Anchor

A private mentor, planner and journal for a dominant in a consensual adult DD/lg dynamic (unlisted at the owner's
request: not in the studio index or README table; noindex; optional PIN lock offered on first open).

- **Today:** a daily nudge "from the old hand", today's rituals, the next lesson, the aftercare window after a
  scene (drop can come a day or two later), and a renegotiation reminder after 90 days.
- **Learn:** 42 lessons in 7 modules (foundations, negotiation, care and structure, presence, scenes, aftercare,
  your own care), written in the voice of an experienced dominant.
- **Her:** a yes / maybe / no list filled in together; safewords and a non-verbal signal; her aftercare needs and
  his; her little space (comforts, bedtime, signs); rules with reasons; daily rituals; rewards; consequences that
  stay "not yet agreed" until she agrees; a scene planner that only offers her yeses and flags maybes; a
  before-a-scene check; weekly check-in questions.
- **Beginner layer:** a four-week "Start here" path through 20 step-by-step guides (first conversation, the list,
  safewords, bedtime and morning rituals, first rules, praise, rewards, consequences, a first little-space evening,
  a first simple scene, aftercare, the next-day and weekly check-ins, debriefs, when she safewords, when you get it
  wrong, her bad days, your voice, caring check-in texts), "How to do this" steps with example words on every daily
  nudge and lesson practice, and a 40-term glossary.
- **Rhythm:** daily, weekly and monthly items (a beginner template included), shown on Today when due, each linked
  to its guide, with a 14-day consistency view and no streak pressure.
- **Journal:** debriefs and notes.

Ground rules built in: two consenting adults; little space kept non-sexual and adult play kept separate; safewords
honoured at once; no breath play or anything on the neck; consequences agreed in advance and never cruel; the
difference between dominance and abuse spelled out. Everything stays on the device. Fonts: Fraunces, Inter (SIL OFL).
