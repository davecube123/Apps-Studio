# Song of Songs

The Song of Songs in a fresh modern translation, with a full commentary, as an installable, offline PWA.

- **Read:** the whole Song in one flow, with who is speaking (She, He, the daughters of Jerusalem, her brothers) marked in the margin, a verse for each day, and your place remembered.
- **Passages:** 25 passages, each with an essence, the text, *Reading the passage*, *In the Hebrew* (two to four key words), *How it has been read* (Jewish and Christian tradition and modern scholarship), *In your life* (love, the body and the senses, inner life) and a question to sit with.
- **Search:** any word in the Song or the commentary, or a verse reference such as `4:12`.
- **Themes:** 16 recurring images (the garden, the gazelle, the seal and the fire…) traced through the poem.
- **Notebook:** select any words, then **Quote** or **Note** (with later additions, dated). Passages with notes get a margin mark; every entry leads back to its place.
- **Introduction:** title, date and authorship, who is speaking, the history of interpretation, how the poetry works, and notes on the translation.

## The text

`data/song.json` holds the translation and commentary (about 40,000 words), both original to this app. The
translation is a new rendering from the Hebrew (Masoretic) text, with English verse numbering (6:13 in English is
7:1 in Hebrew Bibles). Speaker labels, passage divisions and titles are editorial. The commentary paraphrases and
credits scholarly views rather than quoting modern works; the Hebrew text itself is in the public domain.

Fonts: Cormorant Garamond (the poem and titles), EB Garamond (the commentary) and Frank Ruhl Libre (Hebrew), all under the SIL Open Font License (see `fonts/OFL-*.txt`).
