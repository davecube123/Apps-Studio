# Living Window

A quiet window onto an original, generated landscape that follows your real day and weather, with a soft generated soundscape. Leave it open while you read, work or rest. An installable, offline PWA.

- **The real sun and moon:** sun and moon positions are computed from the clock (and your location, if you share it): dawn colours, midday, golden hour, dusk with village windows lighting one by one, night with stars and the moon in its true phase.
- **Your weather:** with "Use my sky", current conditions come from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) (free, no account): clouds, rain on the glass, fog, snow, wind in the trees, storms with lightning and thunder. Or choose the weather yourself.
- **Generated sound,** synthesized live with Web Audio, through a gentle limiter so nothing startles:
  - slow pad chords whose colour follows the time of day (darker in rain);
  - rain in stereo: patter, a low body in downpours, and single drops tapping the glass;
  - wind with a faint whistle in strong gusts; birdsong in three kinds (whistles, trills, warbles), placed where the birds are, fuller at dawn;
  - crickets at night, each chirping in bursts at its own pitch and place;
  - distant thunder after lightning: a long, low, rolling rumble, softer the longer it follows the flash, never a sharp crack. It can be turned off, and it is halved during the sleep timer and silent once the fade begins.
- **Quiet controls:** tap to show; scrub through the day, music and nature volume, a fade-out timer for sleep, keep the screen awake.

Location stays on the device; only rounded coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to fetch the weather.
