# Kasama

A Taglish companion for a person with psychosis and the people supporting him, as an installable, offline PWA.
Two separate spaces, chosen on first open, meant for separate phones. Everything stays on the phone.

**Family space (Para sa pamilya)**
- *Home:* the path to a psychiatrist in six steps, a daily conversation script, notes, crisis numbers.
- *Unawain:* eight short lessons (what psychosis is, lack of insight, early treatment, what makes it worse,
  a calm home / expressed emotion, anger and safety, faith and stigma, recovery and music).
- *Kausapin:* the LEAP approach (Listen, Empathize, Agree, Partner) and scripts for real situations (paranoia,
  anger, "I'm not sick", refusing a doctor, voices, withdrawal, bringing up music), plus practice scenarios with feedback.
- *Tulong:* verified Philippine crisis lines (911, NCMH Crisis Hotline 1553 and mobile numbers), NCMH outpatient,
  what to do if he refuses, and a doctor-visit summary generated from the notes log.
- *Para sa 'yo:* caregiver check-in and self-care.

**His space (My days)** never uses diagnostic labels; it starts from his own goals.
- *Today:* four small, editable daily goals (behavioural activation) and a mood check.
- *Tulog:* bed and wake times with a weekly view and CBT-I-based tips.
- *Music:* pressure-free guitar sessions with a timer, daily ideas and a weekly count; links to Woodshed.
- *Kalma:* paced breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, coping with voices or a sense of threat, one-tap calls to trusted
  people and the NCMH line.

Evidence base and sources are listed in the app (About). It is guidance, not a substitute for professional care.
