# Kasama

A Taglish companion for the girlfriend and family of a friend with psychosis, as an installable, offline PWA.
It is for them only; there is no part of the app for him (his personal space was removed in v11).
Made for non-technical users: a 4-card "how to use" intro, one idea per screen (lessons, scripts, plans and guides
are step-by-step cards with big Back and Next buttons that also work with the phone's back button), the home screen
shows only the next step, Help is a menu of big buttons (Emergency = two large call buttons), and Settings has
three text sizes.
Everything stays on the phone; an optional 4-digit PIN (Private mode) hides it from others who pick it up.

- *Konsulta:* the next appointment (17 Nov 2026 by default, editable) with a countdown and a 7-item prep checklist.
- *Pondo:* a fundraising tracker (target, contributions, remaining), a privacy-respecting appeal message, and cards on
  ways to cut costs: PhilHealth's outpatient mental health package, DOH's free medicines (MAP-MH), Malasakit Centers,
  DSWD AICS, PCSO MAP, common documents, and questions to ask the doctor about cost (checked November 2026).
- *Home:* the next step of an 8-step path (first consult done, prepare the second, funds, notes, treatment, his
  family, a plan), with the old wording: the path to a psychiatrist, a daily conversation script, notes, crisis numbers.
- *Unawain:* twelve short lessons (what psychosis is, lack of insight, early treatment, once treatment starts,
  what makes it worse, a calm home / expressed emotion, anger and safety, suicide risk and asking directly, faith and
  stigma, recovery and music, a ready-to-send explanation for his family, and how friends can help).
- *Kausapin:* the LEAP approach (Listen, Empathize, Agree, Partner) and scripts for real situations (paranoia,
  anger, talk of suicide, after an incident, "I'm not sick", refusing a doctor, refusing medication, voices, withdrawal, bringing
  up music), plus seven practice scenarios with feedback.
- *Tulong:* verified Philippine crisis lines (911, NCMH Crisis Hotline 1553 and mobile numbers), NCMH outpatient,
  what to do if he refuses, a green/yellow/red plan for when things get worse (early signs, actions, hospital, who to
  call), and a doctor-visit summary generated from the notes log.
- *Para sa 'yo:* caregiver check-in and self-care, a section for his girlfriend, and a guide for friends.

- *Bibliya:* a daily verse and a list of 18, in Tagalog (Ang Biblia, 1905) with the English (WEB) one tap away; both public domain.
- *Sa El Nido at Palawan:* direct numbers and step-by-step guides for AICS, free DOH medicines, the PWD ID, Malasakit and PhilHealth.

Design: Fraunces (soft) headings and Inter text (SIL OFL, bundled in `fonts/`), an original illustration and colour world per section, line icons, drawn mood faces, light and dark themes.

Evidence base and sources are listed in the app (About). It is guidance, not a substitute for professional care.
