# Merchant

The art of selling, taught through stories: 30 principles from psychology, rhetoric and business history, each
brought to life by two tales (60 in all), told poetically in plain words, with a note page for every principle.

- **Six books:** The Seller, The Opening, The Desire, The Conversation, The Influence, The Code.
- **Each principle:** a motto, the essence, two tales (Estée Lauder, Ray Kroc, Colonel Sanders, Wedgwood, Tylenol, Barnum, Semmelweis and Lister, Tupperware, Franklin, Columbus, L. L. Bean, the Louisiana Purchase, Edison and Morgan, Pasteur, Otis, Gettysburg, Chanel, Lustig and Ponzi, Nordstrom, Rikyu, the Omi and Sogdian merchants and more), "the deeper why" (named studies and thinkers, with honest notes where findings are debated), four ways to practise it this week, and its shadow.
- **Notes:** a floating ✎ Note button (bottom right) on every principle and tale opens that principle's note, with a format bar just above the keyboard (bold, italic, bullets, numbers, indent). Notebook tab with search; Tales tab with search; backup and restore in Settings.

Content in `data/merchant.json` is original writing; legends, attributed quotes and debated findings are labelled as such.
