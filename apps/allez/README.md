# Allez

Speak and understand French as fast as possible, in 20–30 minutes a day. Built for an English speaker around A2 whose
goals are travel and daily life, dating and social life, and understanding films, series and YouTube.

- **Diagnosis:** an adaptive placement test (about 15 minutes) in five sections: listening comprehension, dictation,
  grammar and vocabulary, sentence repetition (elicited imitation) and answering questions out loud. Items run from
  A1 to C1; each correct answer makes the next harder. Levels for listening, speaking and grammar on the CEFR scale
  come from a simple item-response model, plus weak spots by grammar point, sound and spoken feature, a comparison
  with a can-do self-check, speaking rate, and a rough estimate of the hours to the next level. Retake every 4 weeks.
- **Daily session:** ear training (minimal pairs with varied voices, or numbers at speed), phrases (hear, shadow, then
  say from English, with spaced repetition), a dialogue one step above your listening level (slower, questions,
  read along, shadow, dictation), a role play or a lesson on a weak spot, and one minute of free talk that tracks
  words a minute.
- **Content (all original):** 493 spoken phrases with informal variants (essentials, daily life, social, dating,
  media slang and verlan), 36 dialogues A2–C1, 24 voice role plays, 32 lessons (sounds, spoken French, grammar for
  speaking, strategies), and your own phrases.
- **Media guide:** how to learn from French series, films and podcasts.

Uses the browser's French text-to-speech and speech recognition (Chrome on Android recommended; typing works
everywhere). Progress stays on the device, with backup and restore. Fonts: Fraunces, Inter (SIL OFL).
