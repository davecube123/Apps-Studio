# Trine

A private, passcode-encrypted guide for hosting a consensual threesome well: before, during and after. Unlisted
(not in the studio index or README table; noindex). Content is AES-256-GCM encrypted in `data/trine.enc.json`
(key from the owner's 8-digit passcode via PBKDF2-SHA256; `lib/vault.js`); the plain source is kept out of the repo.

- **24 principles in four books:** Foundations, Before, During, After. Each: motto, essence, a short non-graphic composite scene (Rafa, Isa and Mara), "Say it" lines by moment, "Without words" nonverbal moves, a strategy, "Watch for" signs to slow down, why it works (Lehmiller, Thompson, Perel, Nagoski, Easton and Hardy, Taormino, the Wheel of Consent) and its shadow.
- **One signal system** shown on the home screen: red stop, yellow slow down, two taps silent stop, one squeeze "you okay?".
- **Lines** tab: every line by moment, searchable. **Notebook:** a note page per principle (floating button, format bar above the keyboard), searchable, backup.
