# Ember

Track your cigarettes in one tap and smoke a little less each week, gently, until you stop. No lectures.

- **Log in one tap**, optionally tagging the trigger (coffee, after eating, stress, boredom, others, drinking, work
  break, commute, waking up, phone, habit). Undo from the toast.
- **“I want a cigarette”** opens a 3-minute ride-it-out timer with paced breathing (cravings peak and pass within
  minutes) and your own reasons; beaten cravings are counted.
- **Plans:** just track (a week to learn your pattern), cut down gently (a daily budget that drops by one every few
  days) or quit by a date (the budget steps to zero). A “next on pace” time spreads the budget over the day.
- **Insights:** 7 and 14-day charts with the budget line and week-on-week change, average per day, average gap, time
  of the first cigarette, peak hours, top triggers, money spent and saved.
- **Cravings toolkit:** what to do in the minutes a craving lasts, and if-then plans for your top triggers.
- **Smoke-free mode:** a counter, cigarettes not smoked, money saved, and the health recovery timeline; slips are
  logged without shame.
- A daily nudge from 30 short evidence-informed tips; pointers to nicotine replacement and the Philippine DOH Quitline
  (1558). Everything stays on the device, with backup and restore. Fonts: Fraunces, Inter (SIL OFL).
