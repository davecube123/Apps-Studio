# Noticing

Two minutes a day to see more. An installable, offline PWA.

- **Today's mission:** one small real-world noticing task a day (people, places, light, memory, slow looking, deduction), with a few words to log what you saw. Swap it twice if it doesn't fit your day.
- **Drills:** *Look and recall* (study a generated table of objects, then answer five questions on colors, counts, presence and position: Kim's game) and *Spot the change* (a brief look, a blink, one change to find). Both adapt their level to your score.
- **Journal:** what I saw → what I infer → how sure I am → later, was I right. A calibration chart compares your confidence with your accuracy.
- **Progress:** practice days, drill trends, observation counts.

Everything is generated or original to the app; data stays on the device.
