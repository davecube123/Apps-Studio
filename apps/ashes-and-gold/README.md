# Ashes and Gold

A wordless story about a man's journey from boyhood to elderhood, told through small interactions. An installable, offline PWA.

The story's spine is the Grimms' public-domain fairy tale of the wild man in the cage (*Iron John*), the central story of the mythopoetic men's movement. All characters, scenes, images and sounds are original, drawn and generated in code.

## Chapters

1. **Ahead.** A father walks ahead and never turns. Tap to call, hold to run; the gap only grows.
2. **The Key.** Draw the key from under the sleeping mother's pillow, slowly, or she stirs. Then leave by the door.
3. **The Cage.** Bring the key to the wild man's lock and turn it; each turn, the forest grows louder. Take his hand.
4. **The Ashes.** Sweep the grey ash; it falls again, season after season. Nothing rewards you, until an ember does: hold it, and colour returns.
5. **The Golden Spring.** Kneeling at the wild man's spring, touch the water and hold: the dark wound on his chest turns to gold.
6. **The Four Trials.** Warrior: hold your ground in the wind, never strike. Magician: find the hidden shape among the stars. Lover: meet her note with yours. King: give light to the people around you; each one lit lights you.
7. **The Return.** Walk home to the old father on the bench and sit beside him. Stay, and his hand reaches your shoulder. The lamp from the first chapter comes on.
8. **The Blessing.** Now he walks ahead and a small boy follows, calling. Turn around, kneel, take his hand, and walk on together.
9. **Coda.** One long held touch: every chapter's colour blooms in turn, then a man on the ash, facing the gold.

About 30 minutes. Progress is saved on the device; finished chapters can be replayed from the title screen.
