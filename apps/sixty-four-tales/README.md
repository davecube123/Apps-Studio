# Sixty-Four Tales

A traditional tale for each of the 64 hexagrams of the I Ching, retold to be told aloud, so that a hexagram's
lesson can be passed on through a story rather than a sermon. An installable, offline PWA.

- **Today:** a tale of the day (a fixed shuffle, so none repeats for 64 days), progress, and the next tale to learn.
- **Tales:** all 64, searchable by situation, feeling, place or name (e.g. "impatience", "grief", "Japan"), with region filters.
- **Each hexagram:** the main tale (280 to 420 words, source named), how to tell it (when it fits, a line to slip it in,
  where to pause, how to end), the lesson hidden behind a tap (never said aloud), why the tale is this hexagram (pointing
  to its Judgment, Image or a line), a second tale from elsewhere, and a link to the hexagram in Book of Changes.
- **Rehearse:** the opening line, the beats, the closing line and a timer; hide the beats and reveal them one by one.
- **Cast:** three coins six times; moving lines lead to a second hexagram and its tale.
- **Practice:** spaced repetition (Leitner boxes) in three directions: hexagram to tale, tale to hexagram, situation to tale.
- **Journal:** each telling, to whom, and how it landed.

Content: `data/tales.json` (64 entries). Every tale is traditional or long in the public domain and is retold in new
words written for this app; sources are named per tale. Hexagram names follow Book of Changes.
Font: Newsreader (SIL OFL, `fonts/`).
