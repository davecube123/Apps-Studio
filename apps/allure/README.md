# Allure

A private app for becoming a deep, wise seducer: 30 principles from psychology, philosophy and literature,
each taught through two tales or historical accounts (60 in all), with a notebook page for every principle.
Unlisted: reachable by its link only. PIN-locked like Silvertongue (4-digit PIN stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash; locks on open and after a minute away, hides content in the app switcher, 30 s wait after 5 wrong tries, "Forgot?" erases).

- **Six books:** The Magnetic Self, The Spark, The Pull, The Bond, The Flame, The Code.
- **Each principle:** a motto, the essence, two tales (Cleopatra, Casanova, Ninon de Lenclos, Disraeli, the Capilano bridge study, Abelard and Heloise, the Curies, Eros and Psyche, Layla and Majnun and more), "the deeper why" (named studies and thinkers, with honest notes where findings are debated), ways to live it this week, and its shadow.
- **Tales tab:** all 60 tales, searchable. Today's principle on the home page.
- **Notes:** a floating ✎ Note button (bottom right) on every principle and tale opens that principle's note: a notes-app editor with a format bar just above the keyboard (bold, italic, bullets, numbers, indent; "- " and "1. " start lists). The Notebook tab lists your notes with search. Backup and restore in Settings.

Content in `data/allure.json` is original writing; legends and disputed accounts are labelled as such inside the tales.
